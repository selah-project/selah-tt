# ИСКӘРМӘЛӘР — Selah татар тәрҗемәсе (татарча)

Бу файл **кеше кулы яки карар кагылган аятьләрне** саклый — һәр аятьне өйрәнүче укучы аның
машина ясаганмы, кеше кулы белән төзәтелгәнме яки карар нигезендә эшләнгәнме икәнен һәм
ни өчен икәнен күрсен өчен. Шулай ук аерым сүзләр турындагы карарларны, авыр аятьләрне һәм
әле ачык торган, татар колагын көткән сорауларны саклый.

Һәр язу: аять · нәрсә эшләнде · ни өчен · көне. Яһүдчәсе һәр язуда тора. Язулар бу файлда
**инглизчә** — алар яһүдчә сүз формаларына һәм тамырларына сылтыйлар, һәм тәрҗемәләр арасындагы
уртак эш инглизчә алып барыла. Язулар төзәтелә, ләкин тавышсыз бетерелми — репозиторий
тарихы да шаһитлекнең өлеше.

Текст ничек ясалганын белер өчен `PROVENANCE.md` ны, төзәткәнче — `CONTRIBUTING.md` ны укыгыз.

> **Бу текст хәзер яңадан тикшерелә.** Рәтләре яһүдчә сүзләргә туры килмәгән аятьләр яңадан
> тәрҗемә ителә. Тикшерү тәмам булгач, аның язулары монда өстәләчәк.

## 1 · Words corrected by hand

Every other word in this rendering was written by the machine under the rules. These were corrected by a
human hand, each checked against the Hebrew before and after; each is in the repository's history.

- **`אשר` / `ואשר` rows** · the Russian relative pronoun *который* ("which"), which the model kept writing
  under `אשר` → **`кайсы`**, the word this rendering itself uses for `אשר` elsewhere; the case ending kept
  (Gen 37:22, Num 6:18, 1 Kgs 7:48 among them) · 2026-10-05
- **Deut 12:1** · `החקים` "the statutes" carried the English word *choked* → **`кагыйдәләр`**, in the
  flow and the row · 2026-10-05
- **1 Sam 25:11** · `לא` glossed with Russian *не* → **`түгел`**, the rendering's own word for `לא` ·
  2026-10-05
- **Judg 7:15** · `אל` glossed with Russian *на* → the Tatar suffix **`-га`** · 2026-10-05
- **Zech 8:13** · a Russian *на* → the Tatar suffix **`-ма`** · 2026-10-05
- **Ruth 3:7 `яхшы` · 1 Sam 17:2 `тезделәр` · Lev 19:13 `юк`, `якыныңны` · Josh 6:21 `ирдән`** · Kazakh and
  Bashkir letters written inside Tatar words → the Tatar letters · 2026-10-05
- **Gen 37:22** · a Latin *c* inside `атасына` → Cyrillic **с** · 2026-10-05
- **Lev 13:49** · corrected to **`тиредән`**, flow and row · 2026-10-05
- **1 Sam 2:20** · corrected to **`соралган`** · 2026-10-05
- **The Hebrew above the glosses** · where the Hebrew word shown in a row had been turned into Cyrillic
  look-alikes (`מ` written as `м`), it was restored from the Hebrew text · 2026-10-05

## 2 · Decisions — word by word

Words that looked like errors and were kept as correct Tatar:

- **the vocative `и`** · Song 6:1 `היפה` → *и … иң гүзәл* ("O fairest"); Ezek 7:7 *и җирне яшәүче* ("O
  dweller of the land") · the Tatar vocative particle, not the Russian conjunction *и* ("and"). Kept.
  · 2026-10-05
- **case endings as glosses** · `-на` (dative: 1 Kgs 5:7, Judg 19:22, Lev 1:12, Lev 9:7), `…не` / `нәрсә…не`
  (accusative: Gen 9:24, Gen 37:23, Dan 5:21, 1 Kgs 8:43) · where the Hebrew word is carried in Tatar by a
  suffix alone, the row shows the suffix. Not Russian. Kept. · 2026-10-05
- **`⟨את⟩не` / `⟨את⟩ны`** · Jer 37:15, 37:18; Num 10:29, 10:31 · the Tatar accusative fused to the marker,
  for `אתו` / `אותו` ("him"). Kept. · 2026-10-05
- **`багучы` · `сихерче`** · each place was read: all are verses about diviners, sorcerers or a medium,
  never the prophet — Gen 41:24, Exod 7:11, Exod 9:11, Deut 18:10, 1 Sam 28:9, Isa 47:12, Isa 57:3, Jer 27:9,
  Dan 2:2, Dan 2:27, Dan 5:11, Dan 5:15. `נביא` stays **`пәйгамбәр`**. · 2026-10-05
- **lowercase `алла` / `аллалар`** · for the gods of the nations (Exod 32:8) · lawful; `Элоһим` stands for
  `אלהים`.
- **lowercase `хуҗа` / `хуҗам`** · `אדני` from one person to another (Gen 44:19, 1 Sam 25:24) · lawful;
  `Адонай` when it is addressed to Elohim.
- **`Билгам сүзе`** · Num 24:3 `נאם בלעם` · the utterance of a man, not the prophetic `נאם יהוה`, which is
  **`Яһвә сүзе`**.

## 3 · Hard verses and lens choices

- **Gen 1:26** · `נעשה` … `בצלמנו` → *ясыйк* … *Үз сурәтебездә* · the plural stays plural, as the Hebrew has it.
- **Gen 22:1** · *Элоһим ⟨את⟩ Авраһамны сынады* · the verse does not know Gen 22:13; no foreknowledge.
- **Deut 6:4** · *Тыңла, Йисраэл: Яһвә — безнең Элоһимебез, Яһвә — бер.* · nothing added, nothing removed;
  `אחד` is **`бер`**.
- **Gen 43:11** · `מנחה` in the sense of a gift → **`бүләк`**, not `минха`.

## 4 · Rules held during the work

- **The Name `יהוה` → `Яһвә`** · from the first verse, with Tatar endings on the whole stem (`Яһвәнең`,
  `Яһвәгә`, `Яһвәне`). The spelling `Йәһвә`, a Latin `ə` in place of Tatar `ә`, and a Latin spelling of the
  Name were found and corrected to `Яһвә`.
- **`ו` → `һәм`**, `белән`, or a verb ending (`-ып/-еп`) — never the Russian `и`.
- **Russian words, Kazakh and Bashkir letters, Latin letters** — forbidden in the Tatar text; English words
  inside `⟨…⟩` forbidden. Kazakh and Bashkir letters were replaced mechanically only where the corrected word
  already appears in this rendering; the rest were rendered again.
- **Names by Hebrew sound** — `Авраһам`, `Йицхак`, `Яаков`, `Моше`, `Йисраэл`, `Мицраим`; Qur'anic and
  Russian-Bible forms not used.

## 5 · What the machine leaned toward

The slips that kept returning tell where the model's Tatar was thin:

- **Russian `который` under `אשר`** — the most stubborn; rendering again did not cure it, a hand did.
- **Kazakh and Bashkir letters** in Tatar words (`ғ`, `қ`, `ҡ`, `ҙ`, `ҫ`, `ұ`), and Latin look-alike letters.
- **The Hebrew of the rows turned into Cyrillic look-alikes** — the Hebrew restored from the text.
- **English and Russian words entering the flow** — e.g. Exod 29:23, where English *baskets* and Russian
  *тонкий* stood in the Tatar; the verse was rendered again.

## 6 · Open questions

- **`סל` "basket"** · rendered many different ways across the text (*кәрзин*, *әрҗән*, *себет* and
  others); **Lev 8:26** `ומסל` reads *башлыгыннан*, "from its head" — a wrong sense. Awaits a Tatar ear and
  one word.
- **`⟨את⟩` in the flow and the row disagree** in a number of verses, in every version rendered — e.g.
  2 Chr 26:1, where the flow shows no marker and the row shows two.
- **Row counts off by one or two** · where Hebrew words joined by a maqaf (`־`) were glossed together or
  apart (e.g. Isa 42:10, Zech 14:4, Ruth 1:16–17).
- **1 Sam 25:24** · the row of `אדני` reads *хуҷам*, with the Tajik letter `ҷ` for Tatar `җ`.
- **For a Tatar ear** · the Name's spelling — `Яһвә` or `Йаһвә`? · the personal names by Hebrew sound, or
  the familiar forms? · `пәйгамбәр` for `נביא`, or another word? · the spelling of `Шаббат` and `Мишкан`.
